title: Fair Property Tax preprint on arXiv
---

Our preprint, [Fair Property Tax: Reducing Regressivity in Machine-Learning Valuation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27009), with Haihao Lu, Michael Wagner, and Timothy Sparer, studies methods for reducing regressivity in property valuation while maintaining price-scale predictive accuracy.
