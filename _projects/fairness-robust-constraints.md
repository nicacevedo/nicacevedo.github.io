## Research question

How can optimization constraints support fair predictive models when fairness requirements or modeling conditions must be handled conservatively?

## Project overview

This ongoing work studies fairness in machine learning through robust constraints and robust soft constraints. The project investigates optimization-based ways to incorporate fairness considerations into constrained predictive modeling while accounting for uncertainty in the conditions under which a model is used.

## Context and status

This research is underway at the MIT Operations Research Center. The public description is intentionally limited to high-level motivation and methodology while the work is in progress.
